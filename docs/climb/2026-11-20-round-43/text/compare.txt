Compare WooCommerce POS systems

Sourced, dated comparisons with Square, FooSales, Oliver POS and other WooCommerce POS options, written by the maker of WCPOS.

WCPOS vs Square for WooCommerce

Sales made in Square's POS don't become WooCommerce orders, and their stock changes sync on a schedule (hourly by default); every WCPOS sale is a WooCommerce order.

Read the comparison
WCPOS vs FooSales

FooSales is a yearly subscription per store, and cancelling ends access to its POS; WCPOS is free, and Pro's one-time payment keeps working after the year ends.

Read the comparison
WCPOS vs Oliver POS

Oliver syncs your store to its cloud, and its free plan caps out at 20 products; WCPOS runs inside your WordPress site with no caps, and Pro is a one-time payment.

Read the comparison
WCPOS vs WooCommerce's official POS

WooCommerce's own POS is free inside its mobile app, built for tablets in ten countries, with no offline mode; WCPOS runs in any browser and as desktop and mobile apps, and keeps taking cash sales offline.

Read the comparison
WCPOS vs Jovvie

Jovvie charges a platform fee on each sale on top of its monthly plans; WCPOS charges no transaction fees, and Pro is a one-time payment.

Read the comparison
WCPOS vs Vitepos

Both have a free version; Vitepos adds restaurant and grocery modes, and WCPOS adds desktop apps for Windows, macOS and Linux and iOS and Android apps (beta).

Read the comparison
WCPOS vs YITH Point of Sale

YITH Point of Sale is a yearly licence with separate stock per store, but no offline mode and no card terminals; WCPOS is free, and Pro adds card terminals for a one-time payment.

Who runs this comparison

This page is published by WCPOS, the makers of WooCommerce POS. We obviously have a horse in this race — so every number is sourced and dated, we link to competitors' own pricing pages, and we tell you plainly when a competitor is the better choice. Check everything.

How these comparisons work

Facts come from vendors' own pricing pages and WordPress.org, fetched on a stated date and re-verified quarterly. Where WCPOS loses a category — kiosk mode, vendor hardware, app-store polish — the page says so. Spot an error? Email support@wcpos.com and we will correct it and log the change.
