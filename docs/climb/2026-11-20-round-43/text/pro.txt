Frequently asked questions
Is the free plugin limited?

The free WCPOS plugin is GPL software with no caps on products, orders or customers and no transaction fees; it runs one register, and more registers and the tools listed above need Pro.

What's the difference between Yearly and Lifetime?

The Yearly license is a one-time payment that includes updates and priority support for one year. It does not renew automatically. The Lifetime license is a one-time payment that includes updates forever.

What happens when my license expires?

Nothing switches off. The Pro version you already have keeps working — your register, your data, every feature. What stops is new updates and priority support, until you choose to renew. That is deliberate: renewals have to be earned with updates worth paying for, and it means your point of sale can never be disabled remotely.

How many sites can I use my license on?

Each license can be activated on 2 sites, intended for one live site and one development site. You can see and deactivate your sites in your account dashboard.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, and Bitcoin.
